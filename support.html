Opening the grown-ups area

Tap the grown-ups button at the top of the home screen, then follow the parent gate on screen. Settings, the play timer, progress and the family subscription are all there.

The family subscription

The whole alphabet path and a few games at every age are always free. The family subscription opens every game for every child on the device.

Restore on a new device: Grown-ups, then Plan and payment, then Open unlock page, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Cancel: on iPhone or iPad, Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android, Google Play, your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. You can also tap Manage subscription on the unlock page. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the games stay open until the end of the period you paid for.

The app does not speak Urdu

Instructions are read by your device's text-to-speech. If you hear nothing, install an Urdu voice: on Android, Settings, then Text-to-speech output; on iPhone and iPad, Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices.
