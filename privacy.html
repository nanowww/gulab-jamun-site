Privacy Policy

Gulab Jamun, Urdu for young children. Last updated: 8 November 2026

Gulab Jamun is an Urdu learning app for children aged 1 to 8, made by GhulabJamunLearning. We built it so that a child's information stays with the family. This policy explains what the app does and does not do.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information from children or grown-ups. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

What stays on the device

The app saves a few things on the phone or tablet so it can work: each child's first name (or nickname), age group, game progress, stickers and settings such as the play timer. These never leave the device and are not sent to us or anyone else. The child's name is shown on screen and is never spoken aloud or recorded.

A grown-up can delete all of it at any time in the app: Grown-ups, then Privacy and about, then Delete all data. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

Voice and microphone

Instructions may be read aloud by the device's own text-to-speech. The app does not use the microphone and never records sound.

Purchases

The optional family subscription is sold and managed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see payment details. The store tells the app only whether the subscription is active. Apple's and Google's privacy policies cover the purchase itself.

Grown-ups area

Purchases, settings and links that leave the app are inside the grown-ups area, behind a parent gate that young children cannot easily pass.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or disclose. This is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and similar laws, and with Apple's Kids Category and Google Play's Families policy requirements.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, for example by adding optional accounts, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
